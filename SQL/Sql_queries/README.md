
### Key Insight: Discount Impact by Segment (SQL Analysis)

- High discounting consistently reduces margins by approximately 
  5 percentage points across every segment
- The discount impact pattern is universal — None discount adds 
  7-8 points above segment average regardless of segment
- Enterprise High discount transactions sit at -8.39% margin — 
  the most commercially damaging combination in the business
- Channel Partners maintain 62.51% margin even at High discount — 
  demonstrating exceptional product and pricing strength
- Small Business None discount at 16.67% confirms this segment 
  has structurally thin margins even before discounting

**Business Recommendation:** A blanket policy of reducing High 
discount transactions by 50% would add approximately 5 margin 
points to affected transactions. Given 245 High discount 
transactions in the dataset this represents a significant 
recoverable profit opportunity.
